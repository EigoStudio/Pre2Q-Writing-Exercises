Part 1: Reading - 10 Problems with Electric Cars
Today, electric cars are becoming popular, but buyers should consider these 10 real problems from the video:
  1. Public chargers: Finding a working public charger can be difficult, and charging takes a long time.
  2. Unpredictable driving range: The driving range can change suddenly, especially in cold weather.
  3. Battery degradation: Over time, the car battery slowly loses its ability to hold energy.
  4. Tires: Electric cars are heavy, so their tires wear out much faster than normal cars.
  5. Cost of repairs: Fixing an electric car is very expensive because it needs special high-tech parts.
  6. Unknown long-term reliability: Because EVs are relatively new, their long-term reliability is still unknown.
  7. Battery failure: If the battery completely fails, replacing it costs a massive amount of money.
  8. Resale value: Electric cars lose their resale value quickly, so owners may lose money when selling them.
  9. Insurance: Car insurance for electric cars is much higher because repair costs are expensive.
  10. Price: The initial buying price of an electric car is still very high compared to gas cars.
Because of these issues and hidden costs, we need to think carefully before buying an electric car.
